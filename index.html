Fix: serve this folder over a local server instead of double-clicking index.html. Easiest options:
  • VS Code: right-click index.html → "Open with Live Server" (install the "Live Server" extension if needed)
  • Or open a terminal in this folder and run: python -m http.server 8000 — then visit http://localhost:8000

A separate 60-second timer for the speaker to think before recording — it runs on its own and has no effect on the speaking timer, WPM, or violation detection below.
Violations (fillers, repeats, pauses) are detected and counted the whole time, but the speaker is never cut off — they always get the full time set below. Filler words/sounds (um, uh, like, basically...) are flagged once they pass the threshold — detected both from transcribed text and straight from the microphone's audio level, so a quick "umm"/"ahh" still counts even if the speech recognizer never turns it into text; words the speaker keeps reusing across the whole speech are also flagged as "repeats" — except words that are part of the topic title itself; and a long pause keeps adding extra violations for every extra chunk of continued silence, not just once. The combined violation total caps at 5 — once 5 is reached, the count stops climbing, but the exact moment it was reached is noted separately and the speaker keeps going for the full time.
